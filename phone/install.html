Install Peek Pets

Make your pet a real home-screen app that opens even when the PC is off.

  1. Download your PC's private certificate. It only works for your home network (it's limited to local addresses), so it can't be used to impersonate real websites. Download certificate Safari asks to allow a configuration profile. Tap Allow.
  2. Install it: open Settings → Profile Downloaded → Install. You'll enter your passcode. It's labelled “Peek Pets Local CA”.
  3. Trust it: Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings, then switch on Peek Pets Local CA.
    Checking…
  4. Open the app, then tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Open Peek Pets (secure)

Don't want to install anything? The normal link from the PC works fine in Safari. You just won't get the offline home-screen app. You can remove the certificate any time in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
